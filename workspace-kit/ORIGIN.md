# Where this came from

This workspace system was designed by **Saad Ahmad** and built with AI coding agents under his
direction — the memory layer, the
session handoff, the department model, the verification discipline, the role catalogue, and
the measurement that shows whether any of it is working.

It exists because a lot of AI assistance is impressive in a demo and useless by Thursday: it
forgets what you told it, it says "done" about things it never checked, and nobody can tell
afterwards whether it saved anyone any time. Everything here is a fix for one of those three
problems, and most of the rules were paid for with a real mistake.

If it makes your week lighter, the honest measure is in `progress/` — and the credit for the
system belongs upstream.

You are free to change anything in it. That is the point of it being files.
