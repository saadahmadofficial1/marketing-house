# One page — pin this up

## Starting work

Open the Claude app → **Code** (not the chat — the chat cannot see your files) → open your
workspace folder. Then just say what you want, in normal words. First thing each day, try:

> **What did we do last time?**

---

## Things worth saying out loud

| Say this | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"What did we do last time?"* | picks up exactly where you stopped |
| *"That's wrong — do it this way"* | the correction is written down permanently |
| *"Remember that I always…"* | becomes a standing rule for every future session |
| *"Check that properly"* | it verifies with evidence instead of assuring you |
| *"How much time has this saved me?"* | a real number from your own work |
| *"Don't touch that file again"* | it is frozen; nothing will edit it |
| *"Do it, don't ask"* | fewer confirmations for low-risk work |
| *"Start fresh"* | new session when the conversation gets long and slow |

---

## Three habits that make it much better

1. **Correct it out loud, every time.** "Too long", "wrong tone", "not that folder". Each
   correction is stored, and after three of the same it changes its default behaviour.
2. **Tell it when something is finished.** Approved work gets frozen and never quietly edited.
3. **Start a fresh session for a new job.** Long conversations get slow and vague. Nothing is
   lost — it reads its own notes.

## If you use Terminal instead

`cd ` then drag the folder from Finder onto the Terminal window, press Enter, then type
`claude`. If it says `command not found`, quit Terminal (⌘-Q), open it again and retry.

---

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Replies getting slow or vague | say *"start fresh"* — it will carry the thread over |
| It asks permission constantly | normal on day one. When the prompt appears choose **don't ask again** for that kind of action — the list builds up over the first week |
| It claims something is done and it isn't | say *"prove it"* — that is a real instruction here |
| It forgot something you told it | say *"you should have remembered X"* — it writes it down and that gap gets fixed |
| You hit a usage limit | your work is on disk; carry on later or in a fresh session |
| You are unsure it should see something | say so. It will not send anything outward without asking |

---

## What it will always ask you first

Sending, posting or publishing · spending money · deleting things · anything leaving your Mac.

Those stay yours. Everything else it just does.

---

## Once a month

> **Show me the progress report.**

Two numbers: hours saved on the jobs you timed, and how your output rate compares with the six
months before you started. Both count only what actually happened — so the real figure is
never smaller than what you see.
