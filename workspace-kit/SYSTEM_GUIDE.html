A working method

An AI assistant that remembers you

Most people use AI as a chat window: explain everything, get an answer, close the tab, and start from nothing tomorrow. This is the alternative — a small set of plain files that give the assistant a permanent memory, your rules, and the ability to pick up a job mid-task days later. No extra platform and no lock-in: just files you own. You still need an AI agent (usually a paid subscription), and a hosted agent processes whatever it reads in its provider’s cloud.

Plain text files Works with any AI coding agent Files stay on your machine About 10 minutes to set up

1 · The big picture

You brief in plain language at the top. Everything underneath is scaffolding you set up once and then stop thinking about.

2 · Memory — the part that changes everything

A normal chat forgets you the moment it closes. Here, a correction is written to disk the minute you make it — so it holds next week, in a different conversation, on a different task.

3 · Never lose your place

Chat histories vanish — you hit a usage limit, the app restarts, a week passes. Writing progress to a small file as you go means the next session resumes mid-sentence instead of mid-shrug.

The rule that makes it work: update as you go, not at the end. A file written at the end of the day is a diary. Written continuously, it is a save point — and save points are what let you walk away mid-task without paying to rebuild the context later.

4 · Standing instructions vs. wishes

There is a real difference between telling an assistant something once and giving it a rule it reads every single time. Only the second one survives a long session.

5 · Guardrails — rules that hold under pressure

Every job has things that must never happen. Write them down as absolutes rather than hoping they get remembered on a busy afternoon. These are examples of the shape of a good guardrail — replace them with your own.

Name what can never be automated

Some material must be handled by a person, or only edited in ways that cannot invent detail. Say which, and say it absolutely — no “usually”.

Name what can never leave

Client, staff and unreleased material stays on your machine. Everything above “public” is local-only unless someone senior has approved otherwise in writing. A hosted agent sends whatever it opens to its provider, so keep those files outside the folder it works in, or use a local model.

Check the format before producing

Size, ratio, language, template — verified before the work starts, not discovered after it is finished.

Approved work is finished work

Once a person has signed something off, the assistant never quietly “improves” it. The human edit is the reference.

Honest status, always

Label things working, experimental, or planned. Never let a plan get described as a live capability.

Evidence before “done”

A task is complete when its output has been opened and checked — not when the assistant believes it should work.

Confirm anything outward

Sending, posting, publishing, deleting: ask first, every time. Approval for one thing is not approval for the next.

Disagree when warranted

The assistant checks claims — including yours — and says so when it thinks you are wrong. Agreeable is not the same as useful.

6 · How to adopt it

The folder does nothing on its own. These are the habits that turn it into something worth having, roughly in the order they pay off.

  1. 1Set it up and answer the questions honestly. Open the folder with your agent and say hello — it interviews you and writes its own instruction file. Vague answers now mean vague behaviour later.
  2. 2Correct it out loud, every single time. Not silently fixing the output yourself. Each correction gets written down with the reason, and it compounds. This is the habit that separates a real assistant from a chatbot.
  3. 3Let it keep its own notes as it works. Progress in the session file, decisions in memory. You will not appreciate why until the first time something interrupts you mid-task.
  4. 4Fill in your data policy in week one. Which of your material can never go to an outside service. Five minutes; the thing you will be most glad about.
  5. 5Turn the third repetition into a tool. The moment you ask for the same thing a third time, have it saved as a script. Your tools folder is where your working method accumulates.
  6. 6Make it check before it builds. Most of what you are about to ask for, you already solved once. An assistant that searches its own memory first stops rebuilding the same thing.

7 · Plain-English glossary

The handful of terms worth knowing. Everything else you can pick up as you go.

TermWhat it actually means
AgentAn AI that can read and write files and run commands on your machine — not just produce text in a window. That difference is the whole point. Most agents run the AI itself in the provider’s cloud, so whatever they read is sent there to be processed.
Instruction fileA document the agent re-reads at the start of every turn. Your standing orders. Keep it to about a page.
ContextHow much the assistant can hold in mind at once. It is finite — which is why big files and long chats degrade quality, and why memory lives on disk instead.
Session / state fileA small file recording what is done and what is next, so any agent can resume the job later.
MemoryDurable facts about you and your work, one per file, so the right two can be fetched without loading everything.
Local modelAn AI running on your own computer. Slower and less capable than the big ones, free to run, and nothing you give it leaves the machine.
Semantic searchSearch by meaning rather than exact words — it finds the right note even when you have forgotten what you called it.
ToolA small script for a job you repeat. Written once, then reused rather than rebuilt.

8 · What this is not

Worth being straight about, so nobody adopts it expecting the wrong thing.

Not autonomous

It does not run your job while you sleep. It removes the re-explaining and the repetition; the judgement stays yours.

Not instant

The first week is slower, because you are teaching it. It pays back from roughly the second week and compounds after that.

Not a replacement for knowing your job

It amplifies whatever standard you hold it to. Someone who cannot tell good work from bad gets more bad work, faster.

Not a product

There is nothing extra to buy or log into beyond your AI agent. It is a folder of files, and you own it — which also means nobody maintains it but you.
