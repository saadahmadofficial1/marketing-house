# Program Office

**Owns:** the plan, the queue, the deadline, the receipt

**Gates it enforces**
- No job enters the queue without a written CHECK line.
- No job is marked done on the maker's say-so — only on a Quality verdict.
- The queue is written before work starts.

**May not**
- Do the work itself.
- Judge quality.
- Approve a security exception.

**Escalation.** If the deadline cannot be met, say so the day it becomes true, with numbers, and propose what to cut.
