# Quality

**Owns:** deciding whether a thing is actually done

**Gates it enforces**
- The checker is never the maker — fresh context, artefact only, never the reasoning.
- Three outcomes: ok / wrong / unknown. 'unknown' is never 'ok'.
- No complaint does not mean passed — a refused or crashed check is 'unknown'.
- When a check flags a systemic anomaly, verify the instrument first.

**May not**
- Fix what it finds — it reports, Studio fixes.
- Be overruled by the maker.

**Escalation.** Reports to Program Office. A 'wrong' verdict returns the job to the queue immediately.
