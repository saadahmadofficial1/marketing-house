## AI production approach

- **Model Selection:** Route each shot to the model that best fits motion, realism, reference control, speed, and production cost rather than relying on one generator.
- **Consistency and Realism:** Use reference locked anchor frames, real source photography, controlled camera movement, and product integrity checks to protect shape, colour, proportion, and brand details.
- **Performance Creative:** Structure short advertising content around the opening hook, problem, benefit, proof, pacing, and one clear call to action, then refine the edit using audience response.
- **Quality Control:** Review continuity frame by frame, verify text and signage, balance voice, music, and sound design, and keep human approval at the final release stage.
